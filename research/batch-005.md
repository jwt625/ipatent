# Batch 005: 54 patents (66 -> 120)

Date: 2026-11-25

Sources: the cached candidate pool from earlier rounds (`work/_research/pdf/`, mostly batch-004 reserves) and four research subagents
(landmark lists: NIHF, IEEE Milestones, CHM, Smithsonian, ASME; online forums and articles; topics of the outside5sigma.com blog; Bell
Labs and other industrial labs), about 120 new candidates. New picks were fetched from USPTO (`pipeline/fetch.py --uspto`, 5 s pacing,
no Google requests); every candidate was OCR-inventoried: the printed header confirmed number, title and inventors, and drawing
sheets were read from "N Sheets-Sheet k" / "Sheet k of N" (`~k` = drawing-like pages, for patents that print no sheet count).
All 54 are expired (grants 1849-1994). Selection: reuse first (32 of 54 from the pool), landmark weight, light drawing load.
Titles are Google Patents' rendering of the printed title (a few from the scan); inventors as printed. Figure counts are set by
the figure-table agents, not here.
